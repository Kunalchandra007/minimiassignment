01 · Time

No date arithmetic.

Bare weekdays, “last Tuesday”, “3 November”, and “since then” are resolved in code, in your timezone, before the prompt is built.

02 · Record

Quote, or admit it.

That day’s messages are injected verbatim. If the day is empty, the model is told there is no record. It is not allowed to fill the silence.

03 · Search

One tool, in parallel.

Prices and launches are checked on the live web, one query per entity. A failed search is reported. It is not papered over from memory.
